Frequently Asked Questions

Can I bring my own mouse, keyboard, or headset? +

Yes, absolutely! All our stations feature front-facing high-speed USB-C and USB 3.2 hubs. You can plug in your own mouse, keyboard, in-ear monitors, or controller and download your software profiles seamlessly.

Do I need my own game accounts to play? +

You can log into your own Steam, Epic Games, Riot, Battle.net, or Ubisoft accounts with cloud saves enabled. For gamers without specific game licenses, AuraPlay also provides commercial arena-licensed shared library accounts for popular titles.

How does station reservation work? +

You can reserve your preferred rig tier (Alpha 4090, Esports Pro, Sim Bay, or Console Lounge) directly through our website with instant confirmation. Your station is held 15 minutes past your booking time.

What are the opening hours? +

AuraPlay is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year. Our late-night gaming pass runs from 11:00 PM to 8:00 AM with unlimited playtime and dedicated night discord channels.
