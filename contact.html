Common Questions

Frequently Asked Venue Questions

Everything you need to know about age requirements, peripheral policies, and outside beverages.

Is there an age limit to game at AuraPlay?
Gamers of all ages are welcome during day shifts (08:00 AM to 10:00 PM). Gamers under 16 must have parental consent on file. Overnight lock-in sessions (11:00 PM to 08:00 AM) are strictly 18+ with valid government photo ID.
Can I bring my own keyboard, mouse, and headset?
Absolutely! Every station features convenient front-panel USB-C and USB-A 3.2 Gen 2 ports connected directly to the motherboard for zero-latency hot-swapping of your personal gear.
Can I bring sealed outside beverages?
Sealed water bottles and sports drinks in spill-proof containers are permitted at battlestations. To safeguard our high-end electronics, open liquids and messy meals are not allowed at desk stations.
