Pricing Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if I disconnect or need to leave early?

Simply log out of your session. The system stops your minute counter automatically and credits the remainder to your account balance, valid for 30 days.

Can I share an hour pack with a squad mate?

Yes! You can transfer hours directly from your Gamer Dashboard to any registered friend's gamertag with zero transfer fees.

Can I bring my own keyboard and mouse?

Absolutely. Our front I/O ports support USB-C high-speed devices with no surcharge. Bring your favorite mouse and custom mechanical board anytime.
