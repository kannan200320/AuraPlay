Sensor Science

Does 8000Hz Polling Actually Give You a Competitive Edge?

Standard mice report position every 1 millisecond (1000Hz). At 8000Hz, position reports every 0.125ms. When paired with high-refresh monitors (360Hz or higher), cursor micro-stutters vanish completely during rapid crosshair movement.

However, 8000Hz demands high single-thread CPU performance. On our AMD Ryzen 7 7800X3D battlestations, USB interrupt handlers operate in dedicated hardware threads with zero frametime drop.
